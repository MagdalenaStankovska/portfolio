01About

About

I'm based in Skopje and work across web development and applied AI. I'm a Laboratory Teaching Assistant at FINKI, and my project VitaCoach was published as a paper at ICT Innovations 2026.

My projects usually start from a real problem rather than a tutorial: a Spring Boot service that schedules university exams with 85–90% accuracy, a Gemini-powered RAG assistant that plans real workouts, and a sign-language recognizer built on computer vision. I care about the fundamentals just as much as the AI: clean data models, solid authentication, and interfaces people can actually use.
